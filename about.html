The problem

Strathmore offers Career Development Services and an Attachment System, yet students must still search for organisations, check each opportunity, contact employers and send many applications. Employers in turn receive applications from students who do not meet their needs.

What we are building

A web platform that connects student profiles to attachment opportunities, explains each match and tracks applications from first click to final answer. It is designed around Strathmore requirements, such as BBIT students attaching at the end of Stage II.

What it does not cover

InternConnect stops once a student secures a placement. Supervision, logbooks, assessment and completion are outside the project. It supports your decision; it never makes the final placement decision.

Where the project is

The requirements are written: 26 functional requirements shared across students, host organisations and CDS Officers. The use case, sequence and class diagrams, the database design and the wireframes are done. Implementation has not yet begun, so this site shows the planned interface with sample data.

How it will be built

  • HTML and CSS for the pages
  • Java for login, matching and application rules
  • MySQL for profiles, opportunities and applications
  • Developed in Scrum iterations, with designs drawn in Draw.io

Before you apply

  • Complete your student profile.
  • Check that the organisation is accepted by the university.
  • Read why each opportunity matched you.
