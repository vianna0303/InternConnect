Know why an attachment fits you

Finding an industrial attachment means searching the Attachment System, employer websites and job boards, then working out which opportunities suit your programme, skills and dates. InternConnect brings that into one place.

You build a profile once. We compare it with each opportunity and show you what matched and what is missing, so you apply where you have a real chance. The reasons behind a match are always visible, not just the result.

Who it is for

Students

Keep one profile, see matches with reasons, and track every application.

Host organisations

Manage structured opportunities and review the applications you receive.

CDS Officers

Verify organisations, publish opportunities and view pre-placement summaries.

Before you apply

  • Complete your student profile.
  • Check that the organisation is accepted by the university.
  • Read why each opportunity matched you.
