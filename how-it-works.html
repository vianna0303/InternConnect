The six steps

  1. Create a student profile with your programme, skills and availability.
  2. Host organisations add opportunities with clear requirements.
  3. CDS Officers verify the organisation and publish the opportunity.
  4. InternConnect compares profiles with opportunities and lists the best matches.
  5. Each match comes with an explanation you can read.
  6. You apply and follow the status of every application.

An example explanation

Software Support Intern at a Nairobi firm, for a BBIT student:

Before you apply

  • Complete your student profile.
  • Check that the organisation is accepted by the university.
  • Read why each opportunity matched you.
